Mubit Spectrum Sample

A small sample of the Continual Learning Bench blind-spectrum-monitoring task, scored with the benchmark's published availability-IoU rule. Each scan shows only the transmitters that are active right now — about a quarter of the truth. All three arms get the same scans and the same model, and differ only in what they carry between scans: nothing (memory off), the full raw history resent in every prompt (context replay), or a fixed-size transmitter registry stored in Mubit and recalled each scan (memory on).
